FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes, completely free. No sign‑up and no trial period.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No personal data. Nothing goes to the developer unless you send an error report yourself. Privacy policy

Which sites work?

Most popular video sites work with just a link. If a link doesn't work, open the page in the built‑in browser, play the video and tap "Download" on it. Copy‑protected videos from paid streaming services can't be saved.

Something went wrong?

Settings → Help → Report a problem writes a mail to support@4dir.net with the app log attached.
