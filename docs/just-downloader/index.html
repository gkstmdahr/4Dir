Features

Paste a link

Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, Vimeo, Reddit, Bilibili, YouTube and many more sites.

Download button on videos

In the built‑in browser, a Download button appears on the video the page plays — works on many sites a link can't handle.

Ready for editing

Saved to Movies/VideoRef. "Compatible" mode picks H.264 + AAC so editing apps open it right away.

Works in the background

Keeps downloading with the screen off. Wi‑Fi‑only option, max resolution.

10 languages

English, 한국어, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, Español, Deutsch, Français, Português (Brasil), Русский. Light / dark theme.

Nothing sent to us

No ads, no tracking, no account. Privacy policy

Install

Open the downloaded APK on your phone. If asked, allow your browser or file app to install unknown apps.

A Google Play edition is coming. It can't download YouTube videos and updates its download engine only with app updates (Play policy). Both editions can be installed side by side.
