4Dir

Languages

The 4Dir app is available in 26 languages. It starts in your Windows display language; to change it, open ⚙ Settings ▸ General ▸ Language and restart 4Dir. If your language is not in the list, 4Dir shows English.

This website and the user guide are available in 26 languages; other languages see the English pages. Older release notes may be in English only.
