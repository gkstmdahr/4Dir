Languages

The 4Dir app is available in 16 languages. It starts in your Windows display language; to change it, open ⚙ Settings ▸ General ▸ Language and restart 4Dir. If your language is not in the list, 4Dir shows English.

This website, the user guide and the release notes are available in 10 languages; other languages see the English pages.
